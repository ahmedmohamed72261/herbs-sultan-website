About Us

Our Story

GreenGlobeHerbs is one of the leading companies in the field of exporting medicinal herbs, legumes, spices, and seeds, with many years of experience, an excellent reputation, and outstanding product quality. We export a wide range of products including basil, peppermint, thyme, oregano, anise, fennel, and many others. Our focus is to meet the needs of the global market by offering high quality and competitive prices. We are committed to continuous improvement in our operations to achieve customer satisfaction and maintain the perfect balance between quality and economy.

Our Organic Products

At Herb & Spice Market, we believe in the power of organic farming and sustainable agriculture. Our organic products are grown without synthetic pesticides, fertilizers, or GMOs, ensuring you receive the purest, most nutritious ingredients possible.
